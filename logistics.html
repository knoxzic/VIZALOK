OUR STORY

About the Founder – Booker T. Clark

My name is Booker T. Clark, and Vizalok Logistics was born from both necessity and conviction.

For over a decade, I worked in operations, logistics, and team leadership across multiple states. I took pride in showing up, working hard, and leading teams — often exceeding performance targets by wide margins. But in 2026, everything changed.

While working as a high-reach forklift operator, I was repeatedly forced to perform unsafe tasks despite raising concerns. I was instructed to place empty pallets on high racks with a disabled camera and to clean wet floors in hazardous conditions. When I refused to ignore the dangers, I was met with retaliation. The result was a serious workplace injury that left me with internal hemorrhoids, a sprained ankle, chronic pain, and lasting effects on my health and mental well-being.

Instead of support, I faced termination. I filed a grievance, pursued workers’ compensation, and fought through the system. That experience opened my eyes to how many workers — especially those in physically demanding jobs — are treated unfairly in hostile work environments with little recourse.

I refused to let that chapter define me as a victim. Instead, I chose to turn it into purpose.

Vizalok Logistics was created as my answer. I’m building a company that operates with integrity, fairness, and respect — values I believe every worker and every client deserves. Through reliable medical courier services, I’m committed to supporting healthcare systems that care for patients when they need it most. At the same time, I’m creating opportunities for others — especially those who have been overlooked or mistreated in traditional workplaces — to earn a living with dignity through flexible, independent contractor roles.

My goal is bigger than deliveries. I want to help change the culture around work. I want to prove that businesses can succeed while treating people right. I want Vizalok Logistics to be a company that gives back — by providing stable opportunities, fair treatment, and a path forward for people who are often left behind.

This isn’t just business to me. It’s personal. Every route we run and every contractor we bring on is part of building something better than what I experienced.

If you’re someone looking for a real opportunity to build something for yourself as an independent contractor driver, I invite you to express your interest and create your driver profile below.

— Booker T. Clark
Founder, Vizalok Logistics
