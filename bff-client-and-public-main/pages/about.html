WHO WE ARE

A strategic partner for lasting foundations

VISION  ·  STRATEGY  ·  WEALTH

Our approach combines practical experience with personalized guidance to help clients strengthen their operations, improve financial clarity, and position themselves for long-term success. Whether launching a new business, forming a nonprofit organization, preparing for funding opportunities, improving financial management, or protecting personal and business assets, we provide solutions designed to move our clients forward with confidence.

No two clients arrive at the same starting point, and no two plans should look identical. Some come to us with a business already generating revenue but no real financial system behind it. WHY WE DO THIS WORK

Closing the gap between ambition and access

Numbers tell a story — but too often, the people who most need to understand that story are shut out of it.

Underserved entrepreneurs, first-generation nonprofit founders, and small business owners are frequently expected to compete for funding, contracts, and credibility without ever having been taught the financial language the system runs on. That gap isn’t a failure of ambition or talent — it’s a failure of access.

We started Best Face Forward because we believe that gap can and should be closed. Financial literacy and compliance shouldn’t be privileges reserved for those who can afford a full back-office team from day one. They should be tools every founder has in hand before the stakes get high — before a missed filing costs a grant, before a disorganized ledger costs a loan, before an under-resourced nonprofit loses momentum it can’t get back.

Our work is not just spreadsheets and strategy sessions. It’s about restoring dignity to the process of building something — helping people walk into rooms, applications, and negotiations with their full financial picture in order, so they are taken seriously and treated fairly from the start.

That belief is what the firm was actually built on — not just as a value we mention, but as the story behind why Best Face Forward exists in the first place.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

The principles behind every engagement

01 — Equity

Access is a form of equity

Capital, compliance knowledge, and financial systems are often distributed unevenly along lines of geography, race, and opportunity. We see our role as helping to level that ground — one client, one organization, one clear financial plan at a time.

02 — Education

Education outlasts advice

We don’t just hand clients an answer; we leave them more capable than we found them. A client who understands why a decision matters can make the next ten decisions well after we’ve stepped back.

03 — Integrity

Integrity is not negotiable

Sound financial practices and honest compliance work protect more than a balance sheet — they protect a founder’s reputation, a nonprofit’s mission, and the trust of every stakeholder depending on them.

04 — Community

Small organizations are community infrastructure

Every local business that stays open, every nonprofit that keeps its doors funded, represents jobs, services, and stability for the people around it. Strengthening one foundation reaches well beyond its own walls.

05 — Purpose

Strategy should serve people, not the other way around

Growth for growth’s sake isn’t the goal. We build plans around what actually matters to each client — their family’s security, their mission, their community — and let the strategy follow from there.
